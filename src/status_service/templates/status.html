How this page works

  • A monitor outside YourBot's servers loads yourbot.gg every minute. A failed check is retried, and the monitor tests its own internet connection, before anything counts as downtime.
  • The other checks run inside the platform about once a minute and are reported here. When the website cannot be reached we cannot read them, so they show No data instead of a guess.
  • Uptime is the share of completed checks that passed. A component's figure is that of its weakest check, and overall uptime is the average of the components people use every day. Checks we could not complete are left out, never counted as up.
  • Interruptions shorter than two minutes count toward uptime but are not listed as incidents. Days are counted in UTC.

  • Measurements on this page start on {{ snap.data_since }}, so the 90-day bars are not full yet.
    • {% endif %}
